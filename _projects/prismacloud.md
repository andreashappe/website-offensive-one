[PRISMACLOUD](https://prismacloud.eu/) (*PRIvacy and Security MAintaining services in the CLOUD*) was a Horizon 2020 research project coordinated by the Austrian Institute of Technology, running from February 2015 to July 2018 with 16 partners from academia and industry (grant agreement no. 644962). Its goal was to make cloud services trustworthy through cryptography rather than through contracts: a toolkit of cryptographic building blocks — among them secret sharing for distributed storage, malleable and redactable signatures, and verifiable computation — and a portfolio of eight security- and privacy-enhanced cloud services built on top of them, covering data confidentiality, verifiability of cloud data and infrastructure, and user privacy.

At AIT (2015–2018) I designed, developed and maintained the privacy-preserving multi-cloud storage prototype — the PRISMACLOUD successor of Archistar — including its integration into the project's demonstrators.
