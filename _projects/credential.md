[CREDENTIAL](https://www.ait.ac.at/en/research-topics/cyber-security/projects/credential) (*Secure Cloud Identity Wallet*) was a Horizon 2020 innovation action coordinated by the Austrian Institute of Technology, running from October 2015 to September 2018 with eleven partners from six EU countries (grant agreement no. 653454). The project built a cloud-based wallet for storing, managing and sharing identity information and other highly sensitive personal data, with end-to-end encryption so that even the identity provider cannot read the data in plain text.

The cryptographic core is proxy re-encryption: users encrypt their data before upload, and the cloud can transform a ciphertext for one recipient into one for another without ever seeing the plaintext. This is combined with redactable signatures for selective disclosure of signed attributes and with hardware-based multi-factor authentication, and was piloted in eHealth, eBusiness and eGovernment use cases.

At AIT (2015–2018) I worked on the design and implementation of the data-sharing and identity-management components of the wallet.
