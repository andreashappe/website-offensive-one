The [OWASP Top 10 Proactive Controls](https://owasp.org/www-project-proactive-controls/) is the developer-facing counterpart to the OWASP Top 10: instead of listing what goes wrong, it describes the ten security techniques that should be included in every software project — from access control and input validation to cryptography, logging and secure configuration. It is written for developers and is widely used as the backbone of secure-coding training.

I have been project leader since 2023, covering the 2024 edition and ongoing maintenance.
