The [OWASP Operational Technology (OT) Top 10](https://owasp.org/www-project-operational-technology-top-10/) is an awareness document listing the most critical security risks in operational technology — the industrial control systems, PLCs, SCADA networks and building automation that run physical processes. It transfers the familiar OWASP Top 10 format to an environment where availability and safety matter more than confidentiality, patch cycles are measured in years, and protocols were never designed with an attacker in mind.

I founded the project in 2024 and lead it.
