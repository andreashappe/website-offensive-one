Cochise

autonomous assumed-breach penetration testing of Active Directory networks

Cochise is an open-source prototype for autonomous assumed-breach penetration testing: given low-privileged access to a Windows/Active Directory network, an LLM-driven agent performs privilege escalation, lateral movement and, eventually, domain dominance on its own. It was evaluated against the GOAD (Game of Active Directory) testbed.

Cochise is deliberately small — a few hundred lines of Python scaffolding around the model — and prioritises simplicity and readability over feature count. It is meant as a reference harness: a baseline and starting point for researchers working on LLM-based offensive security, rather than a product.

The accompanying journal paper appeared in ACM TOSEM (Happe & Cito, 2026) (with a replicated computational results report (Happe & Cito, 2026)), and the harness itself was presented in the ASE 2026 tool track (Happe & Cito, 2026).
