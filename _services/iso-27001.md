An information security management system should make your organisation more secure, not just produce paperwork for the auditor. I help you build an ISMS that fits the size and risk profile of your organisation, uses the processes you already have, and holds up in the certification audit.

## Background

I am a certified ISO 27001 auditor and audit management systems for a certification body, so I know what auditors look for and where organisations typically struggle. I combine that with more than a decade of hands-on security work, which keeps the controls grounded in how attacks actually happen.

I do not audit organisations I have advised.

## What I help with

- **Gap analysis:** your current security practice measured against ISO 27001, with a prioritised list of what is missing
- **ISMS build-up:** scope, risk assessment and treatment, the statement of applicability, policies and controls
- **Technical controls:** connecting the ISMS with the engineering reality of your organisation, from secure development to access management
- **Audit readiness:** internal audit and management review, and preparing your team for the certification audit
